# 🎯 How It Will Work

There is no new application. You use three things you already have.

> [!warning] Not built yet (as of 2026-10-29)
> This page describes the **finished** system. Today the study-page generator doesn't exist, so **"study MN 118" gets you an answer in the chat, not a page.** Pages arrive with **Phase 2** in [[Status]], after the design (Phase 0) and the sandhi pilot (Phase 1). Until then, ask the Sibyl outright to *"write it as a page in the Tipitaka vault"*, and treat that page as hand-made, not pipeline output.

## 📖 1 · Reading, in Obsidian (the Tipitaka vault)
Say **"study MN 10"**, and you get a page where each Pāli line shows:
- the **sandhi split**: the merged words pulled apart (*tathāgatopi* → *tathāgato + api*);
- each word's **dictionary form, grammar and short meaning**;
- **Sujato's English** for that line, beneath it;
- links to **every other place** each word occurs in the canon.

As you learn words, they **drop out of the annotations**, so the scaffolding thins as you grow. Later, if you want it, an Obsidian plugin can let you hover over or click any word to analyse it.

## 💬 2 · The tutor, in conversation (the herdr windows)
Ask a Sibyl, usually Nemesis:
- *"Why is this word in the -smiṃ form here?"*
- *"Where else does ekāyano occur?"*
- *"Quiz me on the vocabulary of MN 10."*

The Sibyl looks it up in the local database and answers **with citations**: the line's segment ID and the dictionary entry. It never answers from model memory alone.

## 🃏 3 · Flashcards
Vocabulary cards ordered by how often each word occurs in the canon, so the words that cover most of the text come first. These already exist (`pali-srs`), and will be extended.

## 🌱 The first milestone
**Three suttas you already know: MN 10 (Satipaṭṭhāna), MN 118 (Ānāpānasati) and SN 56.11 (Dhammacakkappavattana)**, glossed, split and readable. If those three are a joy to read, everything else grows from them.
