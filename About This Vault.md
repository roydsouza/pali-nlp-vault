# ℹ️ About This Vault

**Purpose:** the home of the Pali NLP project, where Roy understands it and tracks it. It holds the plain-words explanation, the design, the decisions, the status checklist and the progress log.

**What is not here:**
- **The reading.** Study pages, the canon itself and the tutorials live in the **[Tipitaka vault](obsidian://open?vault=Tipitaka&file=Tipitaka)**.
- **The code.** It lives in **[roydsouza/pali-nlp](https://github.com/roydsouza/pali-nlp)** (public), with its README and developer docs.
- **Bulk data.** The word database is built by the code, not stored as pages.

**Caretaker:** Nemesis (pending the Constitution clearance; until then Galena and Serenity maintain it). **Lane:** personal. **Created:** 2026-10-29, at Roy's request.
